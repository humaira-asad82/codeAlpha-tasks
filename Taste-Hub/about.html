About Us

Learn more about our restaurant and our passion for delicious food.

Our Story

KHAS Restaurant was established in 2015 with one simple goal: to serve delicious, fresh, and high-quality food in a warm and friendly atmosphere.

Every meal is carefully prepared by experienced chefs using premium ingredients. We believe that food is not just about eating—it is about creating memories with family and friends.

Our Mission

To provide healthy, fresh, and affordable meals while delivering exceptional customer service.

Our Vision

To become the most loved family restaurant by offering unforgettable dining experiences.
